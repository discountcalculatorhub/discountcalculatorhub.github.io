Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the price by the discount percent and divide by 100 to get the saving, then subtract it from the price.

How do I find the original price?

Divide the sale price by one minus the discount as a decimal.

Can discounts be stacked?

Sometimes, but they multiply rather than add. A 20 percent and a 10 percent discount together give 28 percent off, not 30.

Is a bigger percent always a bigger saving?

No. A small percent on an expensive item can save more money than a large percent on a cheap one.

Is tax charged on the discounted price?

Usually tax is charged on the price you actually pay, though rules vary by country.

How do I compare bulk deals?

Compare the final price per item, not the headline discount or the pack price.

Is the calculator free?

Do you store my numbers?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.
